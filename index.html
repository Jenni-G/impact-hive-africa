About Impact Hive Africa

Helping good work get heard.

Impact Hive Africa helps purpose-driven organizations communicate their work more effectively, strengthen their visibility and build influence. We use strategic communications to help organizations tell their stories, engage stakeholders and contribute to meaningful change.

We work primarily with nonprofits and social enterprises, supporting them to develop communications strategies, plan and deliver communications activities, and strengthen leadership communications. From shaping the overall strategy to supporting implementation, we work alongside our clients to make sure their communications are clear, relevant and aligned with their goals.

Our approach is collaborative, practical and informed by data and local context. We combine strong communications expertise with effective technology, including AI, to work smarter, improve efficiency and deliver quality work. But we never lose sight of the human side of communication: understanding people, building relationships and creating messages that resonate.

Collaborative
